Supplementary Materials

This page collects the supplementary materials cited in the paper Upper Bounds on the Minimum Distance of Quantum APM-LDPC Codes. It gathers the live table, explanatory notes, representative parameter files, and related slide or manuscript PDFs in one place.

The main paper reports only the best code found for each listed blocklength. The resources below provide the supporting parameter files and explanatory notes behind those reported rows.
